# BlobUI 1.2.0

## Settings and updates

- Settings now groups appearance, accessibility, and app controls into clear pages.
- Automatic update checks and update installation are handled by the native Settings app and installer.
- Updates preserve saved settings, encrypted credentials, custom banners, and files outside BlobUI's managed runtime.

## Accessibility

- Reduced Motion and independent tab, link, and @ mention preview controls are available in Settings.
- Settings dialogs support keyboard focus management and reduced-motion preferences.
